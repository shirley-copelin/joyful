# Lafayette, CA Launch Plan

Lafayette is not just a convenient starting point — it's a strategically ideal one. Here's why, and exactly how to execute.

---

## Why Lafayette Is a Great Launch Market

| Factor | Detail | Why It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Size | ~27,000 residents | Small enough to build network density fast |
| Demographics | Median HH income $200K+, highly educated | High willingness to pay; early adopter culture |
| Activity density | Kids in 3-5 activities each on average | High pain, high frequency of use |
| Community culture | Extremely tight-knit; everyone knows everyone | Word of mouth travels fast |
| Parent involvement | Very high PTA/school club participation | Champion parents are easy to find |
| Natural expansion | Moraga + Orinda = "Lamorinda" shared identity | Built-in Phase 2 expansion with zero new infrastructure |
| Proximity to Bay Area | Early adopter parents, tech-comfortable | Less friction adopting a new app |

**The Lamorinda opportunity**: Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda share a single community identity — kids go to school together, play sports together, and parents move between the three cities constantly. Winning Lafayette deeply gives you Moraga and Orinda almost automatically.

## Anchor School Strategy for Lafayette

### Choose Your 3 Anchors
**Recommended starting trio:**
1. **Burton Valley Elementary** — largest, most active, highest activity density
2. **Happy Valley Elementary** — tight community, great for word-of-mouth
3. **Stanley Middle School** — bridges all elementary communities; parents of 6th graders are often looking for new activities as kids transition

### How to Find Your Champion Parents

**The profile you're looking for:**
- Has kids in 2+ activities
- Is on the PTA board or school parent club
- Runs or is very active in the school Facebook group or Nextdoor neighborhood
- Is the parent other parents text when they need a recommendation
- Personally feels the discovery/coordination pain

**Where to find them:**

1. **Lamorinda Moms & More** (Facebook group, 10,000+ members) — this is THE community hub. Post authentically: *"I'm building something to help parents find trusted activity recommendations from people they actually know — looking to connect with parents in Lafayette who feel this pain. Anyone want to chat?"*

2. **Lafayette CA Community** (Nextdoor neighborhood) — similar approach, hyper-local

3. **Burton Valley / Happy Valley school newsletters** — email the principal or parent club president: *"I'm a parent building a tool for family activity discovery — would love to meet a few connected parents in your community for feedback."*

4. **Your existing network** — You likely already know someone at one of these schools. Start there.

5. **Lafayette Community Center programs** — Hang out at the drop-off for popular Saturday classes. You'll meet exactly the parents you want.

### What to Offer Champion Parents
- Free Joyful Premium for life
- "Founding Community" badge on their profile
- Your personal cell number and a direct line to the product
- Recognition in your launch communications: *"Joyful launched in Lafayette thanks to these founding families..."
- First access to every new feature

## The Lafayette Launch Timeline

### Weeks 1-4: Before the App Exists
- Join Lamorinda Moms & More on Facebook as yourself, not as a brand
- Start engaging genuinely: answer questions, share recommendations, build credibility
- Identify 5 potential champion parents through these channels
- Have coffee with 10 Lafayette parents (your existing network + new connections)
- Build your activity catalog spreadsheet using the seed list above

### Weeks 5-8: While Building
- Set up your waitlist landing page (use Framer, no code needed)
- Post authentically in Lamorinda Moms: *"I'm building something for this community and want feedback from 10 parents. Who wants to chat?"*
- Collect 200 emails from the waitlist
- Finalize your 3 champion parents (one per anchor school)
- Pitch Lamorinda Weekly for a story (offer exclusivity on the launch)

### Week 9: Soft Launch
- Invite your 3 champion parents + their immediate network (target: 30-50 families)
- Do not announce broadly yet
- Be personally available for every bug, question, and piece of feedback
- Goal: 30 families using it with real network connections before expanding

### Weeks 10-12: School Community Launch
- Champion parents activate their school networks
- Lamorinda Weekly story runs (time this to coincide with a natural parenting moment: back to school, summer camp registration season)
- Word of mouth + launch to full waitlist
- Target: 150 active families

### Month 4-5: Moraga Expansion
- When Lafayette NPS > 50 and you have 200+ active families, use the same playbook for Moraga
- Champion parents likely already exist in your Lafayette network (Lamorinda is one community)
- Repeat for Orinda

## The Seasonal Opportunity

Time your launch to align with when parents are most actively searching for activities:

| Season | Parent Mindset | Joyful Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| **Late August / September** | Back to school, fall activity sign-ups | **Best launch window** |
| October | Settling in, finding community | Good for deepening network |
| November-December | Holiday programs, winter camps | Camp discovery |
| **January** | New year, new activities | **Second best launch window** |
| February-March | Spring sports sign-ups | Good for sports category |
| **April-May** | Summer camp planning | **Highest discovery urgency** |

**Recommendation**: If your app is ready by August, launch in late August/early September. If not, target January. Both are natural activity-planning moments for Lafayette families.

## Success Looks Like This at 90 Days

- 200 active Lafayette families
- 3 champion parents who are your most vocal advocates
- A story in Lamorinda Weekly
- Average of 8+ network connections per family
- At least 500 activity ratings in your catalog
- Parents inviting friends without you prompting them
- Your Lamorinda Moms group mentions Joyful organically at least once a week
- NPS > 45

When you hit this, you're ready to expand to Moraga.
